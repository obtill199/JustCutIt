FAQ

          
Lawn care questions.

        

        

          

            What’s included in a lawn mowing visit?
            
Every visit includes mowing, edging, line trimming, and blowing clippings off your driveway, sidewalks and patio. Need extra trimming? Just ask.

          

          

            How much does lawn mowing cost in Wichita?
            
Quotes are based on your yard’s size and layout. Most yards run $40 to $60 a cut, and small yards are welcome. Call or text (316) 559-9535 for an exact price.

          

          

            Should I mow weekly or every two weeks?
            
Weekly keeps fast-growing spring lawns looking their best. Every two weeks works well for most yards the rest of the season, and during dry spells.

          

          

            Do I need to be home?
            
Nope. Just make sure gates are unlocked on service day, or send over your gate code and any heads-up about pets.

          

          

            What happens if it rains?
            
Kansas weather has its own plans. If rain pushes your cut back, you’ll get rescheduled as soon as the ground is ready.

          

          

            What areas do you serve?
            
Wichita and Andover, plus the surrounding area across Sedgwick, Butler and Sumner counties. Not sure? Text your address and Jimmy will let you know.

          

          

            Do you do more than mowing?
            
Yes: yard and branch cleanups, landscape maintenance, gutter cleaning, junk removal and post-construction cleanup.
